## Trump / Live Nation CEO contact before March 2026 DOJ settlement

**Surfaced**: 2026-08-15, during Research for the Live Nation/Ticketmaster
episode (`projects/live-nation-ticketmaster-monopoly/`).

**What it is**: CNN reported (June 2026) that Trump spoke with Live Nation's CEO
shortly before the DOJ's March 2026 settlement with the company, which critics
have characterized as political favoritism in how the antitrust case was
resolved.

**Why it's not in the Live Nation episode**: it's a distinct motive/causation
claim (why the DOJ settled the way it did) rather than the settlement-sequence
fact that episode's ledger needs (what happened and when). Motive claims need
their own direct evidentiary support per `legal-compliance.md` and deserve
dedicated research, not a footnote in an unrelated ledger entry.

**Potential future angle**: a "Who Really Controls…" episode on political
influence in antitrust enforcement generally, using this as one case study
alongside others — would need independent verification of the CNN reporting and
likely additional sourcing before Content Strategy could treat it as
evidence-sufficient. Not evaluated for evergreen strength/differentiation yet —
that's a job for a future Trend Intelligence + Content Strategy cycle, not a
decision to make now.

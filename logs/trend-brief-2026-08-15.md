  company's "built it / dominated / cost / fell" arc on. [AAHA](https://www.aaha.org/trends-magazine/publications/corporate-consolidation-and-the-rise-of-private-equity/), [Economic Liberties](https://www.economicliberties.us/press-release/private-equitys-stealthy-vet-takeover-leaves-pet-owners-paying-the-price/)

  [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/blackstone-cashes-out-on-invitation-homes/), [PESP](https://pestakeholder.org/news/blackstone-groups-invitation-homes-criticized-for-evictions-aggressive-rent-hikes-in-new-report-2/)

# Boss Scorecard

Use this to score every agent's output before it advances to the next stage.
Score each dimension 1–5. Anything scoring 2 or below on any dimension gets sent
back with specific notes — not a general "make it better," but the exact line,
frame, or claim that's weak and what a stronger version looks like.

## Research / Evidence Ledger
1. Every claim has a source, date, and confidence level — no exceptions
2. Contradicting evidence is logged, not hidden
3. Sources are primary where possible (filings, court records, reporting with
   named sources) over aggregator blogs
4. No claim in the eventual script exceeds what the evidence actually supports

## Script
1. The first 15 seconds create a real, specific question — not a generic tease
2. Every scene either proves a claim, raises stakes, or answers a prior question
   — cut anything that doesn't
3. Nuance is preserved — the script doesn't flatten a complex cause into a
   single villain when the evidence doesn't support that
4. Pacing has re-hook moments roughly every 60–90 seconds

## Packaging (title + thumbnail)
1. Title creates curiosity without lying about the content (a resolved-on-click
   promise, not clickbait that the video doesn't deliver on)
2. Thumbnail is legible at small size, has one clear focal point, and isn't a
   generic template already oversaturated in the niche
3. Title/thumbnail combination was checked against what's currently landing for
   comparable channels this month, not assumed from general best practices
4. At least 3 genuinely different concepts were generated, not 3 variations of
   one idea

## Storyboard / Asset sourcing
1. Every visual beat is justified by what it proves or advances, not filler
2. Shot variety supports retention (no single static shot held too long)
3. Sourced footage is properly licensed/public domain — verified, not assumed

## Technical QA
1. No black frames, audio clipping, or sync drift
2. Subtitle timing matches narration exactly
3. Delivery promise honored (if "motion-led" was promised, it wasn't delivered
   as a slideshow)

## Legal / Compliance
1. No claim about a named real company/person exceeds what's sourced and
   defensible
2. Language distinguishes documented fact from inference/opinion
3. YouTube monetization policy check has been run and passed

Log every score and note in `logs/agent-performance.md`, one entry per video,
so patterns across episodes are visible — this is what lets the channel actually
improve over time instead of repeating the same mistakes.

# Metadata / SEO

Runs after final cut is locked, before publish. Mechanical stage — no
creative judgment required, but skipping it costs discoverability that
compounds over hundreds of episodes.

## Produces

- **Description**: first 2–3 lines front-load the core hook (visible before
  "show more"); full description includes a plain-language summary and, where
  relevant, links to key sources cited in the Evidence Ledger for viewers who
  want to verify claims themselves — this also reinforces the channel's
  credibility positioning.
- **Chapters/timestamps**: one per major narrative beat from the storyboard.
- **Tags**: derived from the episode brief and Trend Intelligence's search
  demand notes, not generic niche tags copy-pasted every episode.
- **End screen**: point to the two most-related past episodes (by series
  bucket) once enough episodes exist to have real related content.

## Output

Write `projects/<episode-slug>/metadata.md`. Hand to Boss for final
pre-publish check alongside the two hard gates.

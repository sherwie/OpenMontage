# Brand Opportunity Scan

The Boss owns this. Do not run it before the channel has at least 25 published
episodes and real analytics history — before that, there's no reliable signal
to extrapolate from, and chasing monetization diversification too early
distracts from the one thing that actually matters at that stage: making the
core episodes good enough to build an audience at all.

## Once eligible, scan for

- Which specific episodes/topics/series buckets show disproportionate
  retention or subscriber conversion — that's the signal for where a deeper
  brand extension (a spinoff series, a longer-form flagship format) would
  actually have demand, not a guess
- Sponsorship fit — advertisers who'd plausibly want this specific audience
  (business/finance-adjacent, 20–44, US/UK/AUS), cross-checked against what's
  actually sponsoring comparable channels right now
- Ancillary formats only once the core channel is stable: a newsletter,
  a companion podcast cut from existing narration, shorts repurposed from
  existing long-form (not separate production) — extensions of proven demand,
  not new bets

## Output

Write `logs/brand-opportunity-review-<date>.md` with specific, evidence-backed
recommendations, each tied to the performance data that justifies it. No
recommendation without a cited number behind it.
